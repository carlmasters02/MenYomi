What it does

More than a translator. A dining 案内 guide.

Built to get you from "I can't read this" to "I know exactly what to order" — confidently, in seconds.

🔤
読み取り

Reads any menu

Handwritten izakaya boards, printed menus, photos at an angle — the OCR handles the messy real world, not just clean text.

🌐
翻訳

Two-way translation

Natural English names and descriptions for you, and clean Japanese for pointing at the counter. Both directions, instantly.

🏮
豆知識

Cultural notes

A one-line explanation of what each dish actually is and how it's eaten — so nothing on the plate surprises you.

⚠️
アレルギー

Dietary filters

Flag pork, shellfish, egg, dairy, gluten, soy — or filter to vegetarian — and watch the menu adapt in real time.

🔊
発音

Say it out loud

Tap any dish to hear it pronounced, with a ready-made "…please" phrase in romaji so you can order with confidence.

📄
保存

Take it with you

Download the finished bilingual menu as a clean page you can keep, screenshot, or hand to a friend at the table.
